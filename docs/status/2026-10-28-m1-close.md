# Status: M1 NFC spike closed, 2026-10-28

Closed by the product owner after device testing on a Pixel 9 Pro (Android 17).

## Done

- Writing, reading and verifying sticker links on a real phone.
- Scanning opens the app on the tag screen. With the app open there's no prompt (foreground dispatch filtered to our domain). With the app closed or in the background, Android 17 asks for one confirmation tap, accepted as an OS limit.
- Fixes found on the device:
  - The sticker filter is lost after a write session, so it's handed back explicitly.
  - Stickers are re-dispatched while the phone rests on them, so the session now lasts as long as the screen.
  - Links opened from outside started a second copy of the app, so the app now uses `singleTask`.
  - Write read-back now retries once.
- Domain `plants.pampait.pl` is live with verified App Links; the Worker serves the app-link files and the placeholder tag page.
- Findings and decisions: [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-nfc-read-write.md).

## Follow-ups (not blocking M2)

- The rest of the test matrix: a second manufacturer's phone, the app killed, the phone locked, the app not installed, airplane mode, NFC off, a foreign-data sticker, an on-metal sticker.
- Release-build timings for tag-to-card (NFR Performance).
- Before the production release, replace the debug-key fingerprint in `assetlinks.json` with the upload key and Play signing key.
