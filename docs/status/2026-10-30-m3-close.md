# Status: M3 presets closed, 2026-10-30

Closed by the product owner after testing preset search on an Android 17 emulator: Polish names, typos, shared words and no-match searches all behaved as expected, each in a few milliseconds.

**Exit criteria (AGENTS.md section 11):**
- **Search meets the 100 ms target.** Automated tests check it with 150 presets, and the device demo shows a few milliseconds per search.
- **CI validates the file.** The `presets` job runs the schema, the extra rules, the validator's own tests and the app-copy check.

## Done

- **Preset file, schema and validator** ([ADR-0028](../adr/0028-preset-file-and-validator.md)):
  - The JSON Schema checks every field from section 8, the ranges and the lengths.
  - The validator adds: no duplicate ids or names (compared the way search compares them), sorting by id, a winter interval at least as long as the summer one, and a Polish alias on every preset.
  - The app's bundled copy must match the source.
- **42 presets:** the planned 40, plus Monstera adansonii and Strelitzia nicolai, which the product owner asked for.
  - Care text is in original wording.
  - **116 source links** (university extension services, Missouri Botanical Garden, RHS, American Orchid Society, ASPCA), each rechecked after merging to respond and be about the right plant.
  - **70 Polish aliases.**
  - **All four watering methods**: 38 top, 2 bottom (African violet, maidenhair fern), 2 soak (moth orchid, air plant).
- **Offline search** ([ADR-0029](../adr/0029-preset-search.md)):
  - It ignores accents and case, and searches English, Latin and Polish names.
  - It matches by prefix and by word in any order, allowing 1 typo in words of 4–6 letters and 2 in longer ones.
  - Results are ranked and say which name matched.
  - Entries the app can't read are skipped, not fatal.
- **A debug-only Preset search screen** for the demo, showing results and the time each search took.

**Tests at close:**
- **App:** 291 on Linux, including searches people actually type, run against the real file.
- **Presets validator:** 6.
- **Worker:** 105, and **database:** 87 (unchanged since M2).

## Follow-ups

- **Polish names on retail evidence only:** "Strelicja biała" (Strelitzia nicolai) and "Język teściowej" (snake plant). They can be swapped if the product owner knows better names.
- **Pet toxicity "unknown"** for 4 plants the ASPCA doesn't list: Chinese money plant, maidenhair fern, white bird of paradise, air plant.
- **Lucky bamboo** is often kept in water, which no watering method describes. It's marked "top" with instructions for a vase and for soil. If water culture becomes common, consider a new method value; that's an additive contract change.
- **The remaining presets, up to 120 or more,** come in M7, with the same validator and source checks.
- **A process note:** a broken validator was committed once (`1bf5b57`) and repaired in the next commit (`a51b463`).
